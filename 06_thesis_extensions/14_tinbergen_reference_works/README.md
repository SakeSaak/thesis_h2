# 14_tinbergen_reference_works/

Curated reference library of Tinbergen Institute research relevant to the thesis's methodological and substantive positioning.

## Contents

- **`TINBERGEN_REFERENCE_LIBRARY.md`** — Categorised reference list across five domains: TVP/score-driven methodology (Paper 1 territory), climate/transition risk (Chapter 1-2 + Paper 2), identification methodology (Chapter 10 + Appendix A.14), real-options + investment under uncertainty (Paper 4), and stylistic lessons from the Tinbergen tradition.

## Purpose

Documents how the thesis aligns with the Tinbergen-tradition econometric writing — careful identification, calibrated claims, methodology-with-application, multi-specification robustness, theoretical organisation without empirical over-claiming. Intended both as a thesis-internal bibliography-planning aid and as evidence that the thesis's positioning has been considered relative to the institutional tradition against which it will be evaluated.
