# Paper Portfolio
## Sake Saakstra, MSc EOR thesis-derived publications

This directory contains the work-in-progress drafts of four standalone publications derived from the MSc thesis "Implementation-Risk Differentials in Hydrogen Technology Pathways" (VU Amsterdam, 2026). Each paper extends a distinct contribution of the thesis to publication-grade depth and targets a different journal.

## Paper portfolio overview

| Paper | Contribution | Identification | Target journal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper 1** | TVP state-space methodology for sparse-event hazard models | L4 structural | Journal of Applied Econometrics |
| **Paper 2** | Cross-jurisdictional carrot-policy DiD evaluation | L3.a + L3.b | Energy Economics |
| **Paper 3** | Offtake commitment as $\sigma$-channel mechanism | L3.a + L4 | Journal of Environmental Economics and Management |
| **Paper 4** | Real-options framework with five-channel policy transmission | L4 theoretical | Resource and Energy Economics |

## Shared foundation

All four papers build on the unified theoretical and empirical infrastructure of the thesis:
- The identification hierarchy (thesis Chapter 5, Section "Identification hierarchy and claim structure")
- The five-channel real-options framework (thesis Chapter 3, Section "Generalised real-options framework: five channels of policy action")
- The seven-friction policy taxonomy (thesis Chapter 3, Section "Policy mechanism taxonomy")
- The 80+ analyses in `06_thesis_extensions/`
