## Working title
"The Offtake-Commitment Mechanism: σ-Channel Identification via Cross-Sectoral Heterogeneity in Clean-Hydrogen Investment"

## Core contribution
Pre-FID offtake-commitment requirements reduce hydrogen-project cancellation probability by 11-13 percentage points across five convergent matching estimators (IPWRA, OLS-adjusted, regression-adjusted matching, doubly-robust IPTW, naive IPW). The effect is exceptionally robust to unobserved selection (Oster δ_null = 20.23). 

The σ-channel of the dynamic-investment framework predicts that revenue-volatility-reducing instruments should produce LARGER effects in high-volatility sectors than in low-volatility sectors. We test this prediction directly via cross-sectoral heterogeneity: the offtake-commitment ATT is concentrated in power/heat (-0.30pp), transport (-0.27pp), and substantially smaller or null in chemical (-0.04pp), refinery (-0.02pp). The pattern matches the σ-channel prediction in sign and approximate magnitude.

This is methodologically the strongest identification in the dissertation (L3.a + L3.b + L4 joint), with five convergent estimators, an extreme Oster bound, and a heterogeneity test that directly identifies the operating channel.
