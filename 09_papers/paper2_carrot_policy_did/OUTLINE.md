## Working title
"Mechanism Design over Magnitude: A Multi-Jurisdiction Evaluation of Carrot-Policy Effectiveness in Clean-Hydrogen Implementation"

## Core contribution
Cross-jurisdictional DiD evaluation of five major carrot-policies (US 45Q, EU Innovation Fund, China 14th FYP, UK Track-1, 45V three-pillars NPRM) plus EU CBAM border-adjustment, using S&P Global hydrogen-project database (n=1354, 2010-2026). Methodological contribution: modern DiD triangulation (Sun-Abraham, BJS, IPWRA, SDID, Causal Forests, Deaner-Ku) combined with Rambachan-Roth honest-sensitivity and pre-registered mechanism-falsifiability framework.

Substantive contribution: mechanism design (number of frictions addressed) is more consequential than subsidy magnitude. Two informative nulls (EU IF, CBAM) identify F4 financing and weak border-adjustment transmission as non-binding in the contemporary environment.
